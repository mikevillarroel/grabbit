Privacy Policy — Grabbit

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Overview

Grabbit is a browser extension that lets you download videos and photos from social media pages you visit. This policy explains what data the extension touches — and, more importantly, what it doesn't.

Data Collection

Grabbit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no servers receiving your information. Everything the extension does happens locally inside your browser.

What the Extension Accesses (and Why)

  • Page content on supported sites (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Threads) — read locally to locate media URLs so you can download them. This information never leaves your device.
  • Downloads — used only to save the files you choose to download into your Downloads folder.
  • Storage — used only to remember your settings (download subfolder, Save As preference) on your own device.
  • Network requests — observed locally to detect video file URLs as pages load. These URLs are used solely to fulfill your download requests.

Data Sharing

Because no data is collected, no data is shared, sold, or transferred to any third party, for any purpose.

Children's Privacy

Grabbit does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
